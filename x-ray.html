Settings & data notes
The X-Ray uses Finnhub for live quotes, company profiles, recommendations, company metrics and news when your plan allows them. It also tries Yahoo's public market-data endpoints through fallbacks for additional fundamentals. Free feeds sometimes omit fields or temporarily block browser requests; missing information is shown as unavailable rather than invented. Data completeness falls when fields are missing. ETFs and funds require a holdings-and-fees analysis, so this company scoring model withholds a stock-style rating when the feed identifies one.
